# MindMax Tutoring Design System

## 1. Purpose

This document is the visual source of truth for the MindMax Tutoring website.

All frontend design work should follow this file unless explicitly instructed otherwise.

The goal is to create a website that feels:

- academic
- credible
- modern
- supportive
- ambitious
- established
- parent-focused

The design should feel substantially more refined than the existing promotional material while remaining recognisably MindMax.

The site should not feel like:

- a generic tutoring template
- a children's learning app
- an edtech SaaS product
- a corporate consulting site
- an AI-generated landing page

---

## 2. Audience

The primary audience is parents.

The website should reassure parents that MindMax offers:

- structured learning
- strong academic foundations
- accountability
- ongoing support
- visible progress
- high-quality teaching
- pathways toward selective school and scholarship opportunities

The visual tone should therefore feel serious enough to inspire trust while remaining warm and approachable.

---

## 3. Brand Foundation

The existing MindMax logo is the core brand asset.

The current visual identity is based around:

- strong blue
- strong red
- white

Do not redesign the logo.

Do not replace the core red/blue identity.

The website should modernise this identity by introducing:

- a deeper navy
- warm neutral backgrounds
- pale blue supporting surfaces
- pale red supporting surfaces
- dark slate text
- more restrained colour usage

The logo may be visually strong.

The interface around it should be calmer.

---

## 4. Brand Personality

MindMax should feel:

### Confident
The brand should clearly believe in its teaching and outcomes.

### Academic
The site should look credible and intellectually serious.

### Supportive
MindMax should not feel intimidating or overly competitive.

### Established
The site should reflect that MindMax has operated since 2012.

### Ambitious
The design should communicate progression and opportunity.

### Modern
The website should feel current without following short-lived UI trends.

---

## 5. Core Design Principles

### 5.1 Hierarchy over decoration

Use:
- typography
- spacing
- contrast
- layout
- imagery

before relying on decorative effects.

### 5.2 Restraint

Red and blue are both strong colours.

They should not compete constantly.

Use neutral space to allow the brand colours to feel intentional.

### 5.3 Editorial composition

Avoid repetitive component grids.

Use:
- asymmetric layouts
- large type
- image compositions
- whitespace
- varying section structure

### 5.4 Evidence before claims

Where possible, visually prioritise:
- results
- process
- program structure
- support model
- parent communication

rather than vague marketing statements.

### 5.5 Parent-first clarity

A parent should be able to understand within seconds:

- who MindMax is for
- what MindMax teaches
- how classes work
- what support exists
- why they should trust MindMax
- how to enquire

---

## 6. Reference Direction

### Contour Education

Contour is the primary visual reference.

Take inspiration from:

- warm off-white canvas
- oversized typography
- strong hierarchy
- asymmetric layouts
- editorial photography
- large result statistics
- alternating open and dark sections
- varied section structures
- visual learning journeys
- custom illustration language
- prominent social proof
- generous whitespace

Do not copy:
- exact layouts
- illustration style
- lime accent colour
- wording
- proprietary visual compositions

### HZ Tutoring

Take selective inspiration from:

- minimal navigation
- strong whitespace
- direct CTAs
- simple layouts
- visible social proof

Do not copy:
- the green palette
- the visual identity
- specific layouts

MindMax should ultimately feel more polished and cohesive.

## 9. Colour Balance

Target approximately:

- 60% neutral
- 30% blue family
- 10% red

Avoid:

- alternating bright-red and bright-blue sections
- large red backgrounds throughout the page
- multiple accent colours competing simultaneously

Red should feel deliberate.

## 12. Typography Rules

Use typography as a visual device.

Prefer:
- short headings
- strong line breaks
- left alignment
- large scale contrast
- selective red emphasis

Avoid:
- centring every section
- long marketing headlines
- excessive coloured words
- overly playful fonts

---

## 13. Copy Style

Copy should feel:

- concise
- specific
- credible
- confident
- parent-focused

Avoid generic phrases such as:

- Unlock your child's full potential
- Empowering students to succeed
- Your journey to success starts here
- Where learning meets excellence

unless specifically approved.

Existing MindMax language worth preserving includes:

- The class ends. Our support doesn't.
- Progress tracked. Parents informed.
- Beyond Scores. Build the Skills. Achieve the Results.

Do not invent unsupported claims.

---

## 14. Supported Business Claims

Current approved claims include:

- Established since 2012
- Australian-based teachers
- 100+ Selective School Offers
- 45+ School Scholarships

These should be presented as historical achievements, not guarantees.

---

## 15. Layout System

Use a strong responsive grid.

Suggested maximum content width:
1240–1320px

Desktop:
12-column grid

Tablet:
flexible 8-column equivalent

Mobile:
single-column or intentional split layouts

Do not force every section into the same container structure.

---

## 16. Section Rhythm

The homepage should alternate visual language.

Example rhythm:

warm open hero

→ dark navy feature section

→ open program section

→ editorial curriculum layout

→ high-impact results section

→ timeline / learning journey

→ parent progress section

→ testimonials

→ FAQ

→ strong final CTA

Avoid repetitive section structures.

---

## 17. Spacing

Desktop major sections:
approximately 120–180px vertical padding

Tablet:
approximately 96–128px

Mobile:
approximately 72–96px

Spacing should vary according to the section.

Do not mechanically apply identical padding everywhere.

## 19. Button Shape

Preferred radius:
8–10px

Avoid excessive pill buttons.

Buttons should feel substantial but not oversized.

---

## 20. Cards

Use cards only when content benefits from containment.

Preferred:
- thin border
- light surface contrast
- subtle shadow if required
- moderate radius

Avoid:
- every section becoming cards
- repeated 3-card grids
- large floating shadow boxes
- excessive nested cards

---

## 21. Photography

Prioritise:

1. real MindMax tutors
2. real MindMax students
3. real MindMax classrooms
4. real learning interactions
5. realistic curated stock only where necessary

Good photography should show:
- teaching
- writing
- discussion
- tutoring
- feedback
- parent/student review
- online classes

Avoid:
- children posing directly at camera
- exaggerated smiles
- thumbs-up stock photography
- obviously AI-generated students

---

## 22. Image Treatment

Photography should often be integrated into composition rather than shown as simple rectangles.

Use:
- rounded image masks
- arches
- asymmetric crops
- overlapping geometry
- subtle brand shapes
- editorial image groupings

Do not over-decorate images.

---

## 23. MindMax Geometry

Use the MindMax M as subtle visual inspiration.

Possible motifs:
- soft arches
- central valleys
- mirrored forms
- notches
- chevrons
- rounded vertical elements

Use these abstractly.

Do not repeat literal M graphics throughout the site.

---

## 24. Illustration System

If illustrations are used:

Palette:
- navy
- brand blue
- white / ivory
- small red accent

Style:
- clean
- editorial
- flat / line hybrid
- academic
- modern

Do not use emojis as production icons.

Do not mix illustration styles.

---

## 25. Motion

Motion should support hierarchy, not distract.

Allowed:
- subtle fade/translate
- image reveals
- hover transitions
- accordion transitions
- restrained number animations
- navigation transitions

Avoid:
- constant floating
- excessive parallax
- bouncing buttons
- every element animating on scroll
- long entrance delays

Respect `prefers-reduced-motion`.

---

## 26. Navigation

Navigation should be:

- sticky
- compact
- clean
- clearly structured

Possible links:

- Programs
- How It Works
- Results
- About
- FAQs
- Locations

Primary CTA:
Enquire Now

Do not make every navigation link a pill.

---

## 27. Mobile Design

Mobile should be designed intentionally.

Do not simply stack desktop sections.

Pay particular attention to:

- hero image composition
- typography
- result statistics
- timelines
- navigation
- spacing
- CTA sizing
- image cropping
- horizontal overflow

Test at:

375px
430px
768px
1024px
1440px

---

## 28. Accessibility

Ensure:

- semantic HTML
- logical heading hierarchy
- visible focus states
- keyboard navigation
- accessible accordions
- accessible interactive components
- meaningful alt text
- strong colour contrast
- adequate touch targets
- reduced-motion support

Do not rely solely on red/blue colour to indicate meaning.

---

## 29. Anti-Template Rules

The site must not look like generic AI output.

Avoid:

- endless 3-card sections
- glassmorphism
- large purple gradients
- glowing blobs
- floating dashboards
- excessive pills
- icon-in-circle benefit cards everywhere
- excessive shadows
- repetitive centred sections
- random animation
- identical section layouts
- generic SaaS hero composition

The site should feel art-directed.

---

## 30. Visual Quality Checklist

Before approving any major frontend section, ask:

- Does it feel recognisably MindMax?
- Does it feel established?
- Is red being used selectively?
- Is the navy providing enough authority?
- Is there enough warm neutral space?
- Is the typography strong enough?
- Does the composition feel editorial?
- Are there too many cards?
- Are too many sections centred?
- Does this feel like an AI-generated template?
- Is the parent decision journey clear?
- Are outcomes and support visible?

If not, refine before moving on.
